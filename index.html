The journey

Program Structure

A transparent route from registration to an internationally recognised qualification.

  1. STAGE 01

    REGISTRATION

    Please fill out the form to register your interest in our program.

  2. STAGE 02

    APPLICATION

    All registrants will receive an application form. Applications will evaluate the logical thinking capacity and passion for technology of the applicants.

  3. STAGE 03

    WORKSHOPS

    All registrants will receive an application form. Applications will evaluate the logical thinking capacity and passion for technology of the applicants.

  4. STAGE 04

    INTERVIEWS

    Selected candidates will be invited for interviews. These interviews aim to identify the most suitable candidates for the course. All successful candidates will be eligible for a 100% scholarship.

  5. STAGE 05

    TRAINING PROGRAMME

    Successful candidates can join our 9 months full-time course at our campus in Jaffna.

    (STARTING MARCH 2024)
  6. STAGE 06

    Funded Internationally Recognised Qualification

    Industry Ready candidates prepared for – Azure Developer Associate Certification (AZ204). Exam fees funded for eligible candidates.

    (STARTING MARCH 2024)

Our Story

Later, Shantha engaged with his childhood friend Dr. Neil Anthony-Pillai in a discussion about this transformative incident. Together, they unearthed a grander vision for Sri Lanka.
