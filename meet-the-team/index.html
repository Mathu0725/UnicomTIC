Leadership

Meet the Team

Our founding Directors, proud children of Sri Lanka, boast a collective experience of over 80 years in the global IT industry. Their visionary leadership is supported by a team of distinguished professors, accomplished leaders, and tech pioneers from diverse backgrounds and nationalities.

Co Founder & Executive Director

Shantha Ruban

W. ShanthaRuban, a visionary and an eminent figure acclaimed among the World’s 100 Global Tech Innovators stands as a visionary serial tech entrepreneur and the Founder of 5 tech companies in the UK. He is currently serving as the Group CEO of Programus Limited UK, developing world-class solutions for some of the worlds leading conglomerates, while also shaping the digital landscape with cutting-edge technology.

Co Founder & Chief Training and Innovation Director

Dr. Neil Anthony-Pillai

Dr. Neil Anthony-Pillai’s IT career started at NIBM in 1981 and has spanned over 40 years. He has worked for multiple blue-chip companies and Médecins Sans Frontières on projects across the world. Having always had a passion for training he has designed and delivered training in both Information Technology and Medicine.

Chief Technology Officer

Jon Guffaston

Renowned as one of the world’s foremost technicians, Jon brings a wealth of technical expertise to Unicom TIC. His primary focus is on preparing every student for the demands of the industry, guaranteeing that certificates are endorsed directly from the CTO’s office in the UK.

Advisor

Prof. Dhayalan Velauthapillai

Professor Dhayalan Velauthapillai is attached to the Faculty of Engineering and Science, Western Norway University of Applied Sciences (HVL), Norway. He heads the international research group on Advanced Nanomaterials for Clean Energy and Health Applications (ANCEHA).

Advisor

S. Renganathan

With an illustrious banking career spanning over four decades, Sivakrishnarajah Renganathan has emerged as a luminary in the industry. Notably, he served as the Managing Director / Chief Executive Officer of the globally recognized Commercial Bank of Ceylon PLC.

Technologist & Head of programs

Mayooran Somasundaram

As our Technologist and Senior Tech Lead, Mayooran is instrumental in imparting practical knowledge and industry best practices to our students. His extensive experience and hands-on approach contribute significantly to the development of well-rounded tech professionals.

Operations Manager

Duwarahan Balakrishnan

Duwarahan Balakrishnan takes charge of the day-to-day operations at Unicom TIC, ensuring seamless functioning and a conducive learning environment for our students.

Strategic Director

Anushyanthan Selvanayagam

Anushan assumes a pivotal role as the Facilitator. His wealth of experience and expertise adds significant value to the team, contributing directly to the success of our programs.
