Inauguration - 2024

On March 16, 2024, the inauguration ceremony of Unicom TIC marked a significant milestone in empowering tech innovators in Sri Lanka. Attended by distinguished guests and industry leaders, the event featured speeches from Shantha Ruban, Founder and Group CEO, and Dr. Neil Anthony-Pillai, co-founder and tech veteran, who highlighted the center’s mission to bridge the gap between education and industry.

The ceremony included a ribbon-cutting event, symbolizing the official opening of the center. Guests toured the modern facilities, which feature advanced classrooms and collaborative workspaces designed to enhance the learning experience.
