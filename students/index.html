Admissions

How to Join Unicom TIC

  1. STAGE 1

    APPLICATION

    Everyone registering an interest will receive an application form. This is designed to evaluate the logical thinking capacity and passion for technology of the applicants.

  2. STAGE 2

    WORKSHOPS & INTERVIEWS

    Selected candidates from stage – 1 will be invited to attend a workshop and interview to be held across the regions designed to identify the suitable candidates for the course. All suitable candidates are eligible for 100% Scholarship.

  3. STAGE 3

    TRAINING PROGRAM

    Successful candidates from the selection process will be awarded a 100% scholarship to attend the 9-month program at our UnicomTic Training and Innovation Centre in Jaffna.

Your questions answered

Students' FAQs

What is the Full Scholarship Program?

The Full Scholarship Program is a comprehensive nine-month, face-to-face coaching program that provides top-tier training in the tech field. It covers all costs associated with training, allowing passionate individuals to focus solely on honing their skills.

Is the Full Scholarship Program entirely free of cost?

Yes, the program is a 100% scholarship initiative. It covers all expenses related to coaching and training.

What qualifications do I need to apply for the Full Scholarship Program?

We welcome passionate individuals from diverse backgrounds. Specific eligibility criteria will be detailed during the application process.

How long does it take to complete the program?

The Full Scholarship Program is designed to be completed within nine months, ensuring rapid skill development.

What coaching methods are employed in the program?

The program combines various dynamic coaching methods, including practical sessions, workshops, and lectures delivered by industry experts.

Who are the instructors in the Full Scholarship Program?

Our instructors are seasoned experts with extensive industry experience. They are dedicated to providing you with the best possible training and preparing you for success in the digital landscape.
