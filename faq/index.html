Frequently Asked Questions

Answers about the Unicom TIC scholarship program, learning journey, and lecturer opportunities.

Your questions answered

Students' FAQs

What is the Full Scholarship Program?

The Full Scholarship Program is a comprehensive nine-month, face-to-face coaching program that provides top-tier training in the tech field. It covers all costs associated with training, allowing passionate individuals to focus solely on honing their skills.

Is the Full Scholarship Program entirely free of cost?

Yes, the program is a 100% scholarship initiative. It covers all expenses related to coaching and training.

What qualifications do I need to apply for the Full Scholarship Program?

We welcome passionate individuals from diverse backgrounds. Specific eligibility criteria will be detailed during the application process.

How long does it take to complete the program?

The Full Scholarship Program is designed to be completed within nine months, ensuring rapid skill development.

What coaching methods are employed in the program?

The program combines various dynamic coaching methods, including practical sessions, workshops, and lectures delivered by industry experts.

Who are the instructors in the Full Scholarship Program?

Our instructors are seasoned experts with extensive industry experience. They are dedicated to providing you with the best possible training and preparing you for success in the digital landscape.

Your questions answered

Lecturers' FAQs

How do I apply to become a lecturer at Unicom TIC?

To apply, simply visit our website and navigate to the “Lecturers” section. There, you’ll find the application form and detailed instructions on how to proceed.

What qualifications are required to become a lecturer?

We prefer candidates with a minimum of a bachelor’s degree in a relevant field and substantial industry experience. A minimum of 4 years of coaching experience is mandatory.

What subjects and courses will I be teaching?

The subjects and courses will be related to cutting-edge technology skills, including programming languages, software development, data science, and more.

How often are lectures conducted?

Lectures are typically scheduled on specific days of the week, with options for both full-time and part-time positions. The exact schedule will be discussed during the interview process.

Can I conduct workshops or additional learning sessions?

Yes, we encourage lecturers to organize workshops and extra sessions to enhance students’ understanding and practical skills.

Are there opportunities for career growth within Unicom TIC?

Yes, exceptional lecturers may have the chance to take on leadership roles or contribute to curriculum development and enhancement.
